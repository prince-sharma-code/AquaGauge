# Asset provenance

The files in `demo_images/` and `reference_graphics/` were extracted from the team's uploaded `CodeStorm_AquaGauge.pptx` on 26 October 2026. The PPTX did not include complete per-image source/licence metadata. Use them as local demo inputs that the team already has; verify source and permission before redistributing them or publishing a public repo.

These are not a labelled training set, not independent test data, and not ground-truth depth measurements.
